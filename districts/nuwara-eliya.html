Nuwara Eliya District

නුවරඑළියநுவரெலியா

The high tea country, with cool air, old bungalows and the plateau of Horton Plains.

See Nuwara Eliya on the 3D map

Nuwara Eliya is the highest town on the main tourist trail. Tea estates cover the slopes around it, and many factories welcome visitors. Horton Plains starts before dawn and ends at World's End, where the clouds often roll in by 10 am. Bring a warm layer for the evenings.
