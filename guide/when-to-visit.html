When to visit Sri Lanka

There is no bad month to visit. Two monsoons hit different coasts at different times, so the trick is to match your route to the season.

The short answer

December to April is the main season for the south and west coasts, the hill country and the cultural sites. May to September is the season for the east coast. The cultural triangle works for most of the year.

The two monsoons

The southwest monsoon brings rain to the west and south coasts and the hills from about May to September. The northeast monsoon brings rain to the north and east from about November to January. October and November can be wet almost everywhere.

Rain often comes as a heavy afternoon shower rather than all day. Seas are a bigger issue than rain: many beaches are not safe for swimming in their monsoon months.

Events to plan around

  • Sinhala and Tamil New Year in mid April. Many families travel and some shops close.
  • Esala Perahera in Kandy, in July or August. Wonderful to see. Book rooms early.
  • Whale season off Mirissa, roughly November to April.
  • The elephant gathering at Minneriya, roughly July to October.
