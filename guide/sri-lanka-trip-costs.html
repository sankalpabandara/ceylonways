How much does a Sri Lanka trip cost?

It depends most on where you sleep and whether you have a private driver. Here are sample numbers from our planner for the 7 day Highlights route.

Where the money goes

Stays

The biggest cost. A good homestay can be a third of the price of a boutique hotel, and the welcome is often warmer. Rates rise in December to April on the coasts and in the hills.

A car and driver

A private driver charges by the day, plus distance. It is the most comfortable way to travel and lets you stop when you want. For a slower trip with few moves, transfers between towns cost less.

Entry fees

Major sites charge foreign visitors much more than locals. Sigiriya and the national park safaris are the biggest of these. Check current fees before you go, as they change.

Food

Local rice and curry is cheap. Hotel restaurants cost more. Half board is good value in remote places with few restaurants.

Ways to spend less

  • Mix one or two special stays with good homestays.
  • Take the train for part of the hill country.
  • Stay longer in fewer places. Every move costs a day of driving.
  • Travel in the shoulder months, like May or November, if your route suits the weather.
