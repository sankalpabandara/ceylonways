Travel Guide

Sri Lanka entry requirements

Rules, fees and visa free schemes change often. Always check the official sources below before you travel.

Passport

Your passport should be valid for at least six months from the day you arrive, with a blank page for the entry stamp.

ETA or visa

Most visitors need an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) or a visa before they fly. Some nationalities have been offered free or visa free entry at different times. Apply only through the official Sri Lanka ETA website and be careful of look alike sites that charge extra.

Official sources: the Department of Immigration and Emigration of Sri Lanka at immigration.gov.lk, and the ETA portal at eta.gov.lk.

Health

Talk to a travel clinic about vaccines a few weeks before you go. Use insect repellent, as dengue is present in Sri Lanka.

Insurance

Get travel insurance that covers medical care and any activities you plan, like surfing or hiking.

Money

The currency is the Sri Lankan rupee. Cards work in hotels and larger shops. Carry cash for tuk tuks, small shops and temple donations.
