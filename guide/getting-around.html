Travel Guide

Getting around Sri Lanka

Distances are short but roads are slow, often winding and busy. Plan on 30 to 40 km an hour in the hills.

Arriving

Most visitors land at Bandaranaike International Airport near Colombo, about 30 minutes from Negombo. After a long flight, a first night in Negombo is easier than a four hour drive.

Private car and driver

The most popular way to tour. Your driver knows the roads, carries your bags and can wait while you visit sites. A car takes up to three people with luggage. Larger groups need a van.

Trains

The hill country line from Kandy to Ella is one of the great train rides. Reserved seats open some weeks ahead and sell out in busy months. Unreserved tickets are cheap, but the carriages get crowded.

Tuk tuks and taxis

Good for short trips in towns. Agree the fare first, or use a ride app where it works, mostly around Colombo and Kandy.

Buses

Very cheap and they go everywhere, but they are slow and crowded, with little room for bags.
