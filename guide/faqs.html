Questions about planning

Is this a real booking site?

Not yet. Ceylonways is a working prototype. You can plan a trip, save it and download it. Prices are sample estimates. When the service opens, a person will check your plan with real hotels and drivers before anything is booked.

Can I change a package?

Yes, all of it. Change dates, travellers, stays, places, nights, activities, pace and transport. If a change affects other days, the planner shows how before you apply it, and you can undo.

Are the hotel ratings from Google?

No. The planner uses sample ratings for sample stay types. Real Google ratings will show with a link to the source and the right attribution once we connect them.

What is the rating rule?

You pick a minimum rating and review count. Stays below your rule are marked. If no stay in a place meets it, the planner tells you and lets you decide whether to widen it.

What if my budget is too small?

The planner shows how far over you are and which changes would save the most. If no version of the trip fits, it says so and suggests a shorter trip, simpler stays or different dates. It never drops things without asking.

Does the price include flights?

No. You can tell the planner that your budget includes flights and enter what you expect to pay. We take that off your budget before comparing.

How do I save my plan?

Press Save. The plan stays in this browser on this device. You can also copy a share link or download the itinerary as a text file or print it to PDF.

Do I need an account?

No. You can plan, save and download without signing up.
