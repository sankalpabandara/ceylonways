Where to stay in Sri Lanka

We want every stay to be one you are glad you picked. Here is how we choose, and what is real today.

Sample data. Live hotel inventory, Google ratings and offers are not connected yet. The planner uses sample stay types with sample ratings so you can try the choices.

Hotels and resorts

Pools, restaurants and family rooms. Good for families and longer beach stays.

6 sample stays of this type in the planner, in Sigiriya, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Mirissa, Galle, Trincomalee.

Boutique stays

Small hotels with character: tea bungalows, merchant houses, clifftop rooms.

7 sample stays of this type in the planner, in Negombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Mirissa, Galle, Trincomalee.

Villas

Private space for couples, families or friends. Some come with a cook.

Eco lodges

Near the forest or the parks, often with guides and walks from the door.

How we choose stays

We look at these together, not one at a time:

  • Rating out of five, and how many reviews it is based on
  • How recent the reviews are
  • Complaints that keep coming up, when there are enough reviews to tell
  • Location and how it fits your route
  • The total price for your dates and room setup
  • Meals, cancellation terms and offers

Ratings and star classes are different

A Google Maps rating is what guests scored, out of five. A star class is an official hotel category based on facilities. A small guesthouse can have a 4.9 rating and no star class. We show both, separately.

The rating rule

You choose the minimum rating and review count in the planner. Our founder first asked for 5.0 only. That rule is available, but it often leaves only places with very few reviews, which tell you less. If your rule leaves no stays in a place, the planner says so and lets you decide whether to widen it. It never lowers your rule on its own.

Small review counts

A place with 30 reviews might be wonderful, but 30 reviews are not enough to say it has few problems. We mark small samples so you can judge.

Who pays us

Every suggestion in the planner today is editorial. We have no sponsored listings or commission deals yet. When we do, each one will be labelled.
