Explore Sri Lanka

All 25 districts

Sri Lanka has 25 districts, and climate and scenery can change a lot within one. Browse them here, or explore them on the 3D map.

  • AmparaEastern ProvinceA long stretch of the east coast and its rice plains, best known for the surf town of Arugam Bay.1 place
  • AnuradhapuraNorth Central ProvinceThe first great capital of Sri Lanka, set among ancient reservoirs in the dry north.3 places
  • BadullaUva ProvinceThe eastern hill country, home to Ella, its tea slopes and some of the island's tallest waterfalls.4 places
  • BatticaloaEastern ProvinceA lagoon city and calm bays on the east coast, with its best weather in the middle of the year.2 places
  • ColomboWestern ProvinceThe commercial capital, where most trips begin or end, with temples, markets and a seaside promenade.2 places
  • GalleSouthern ProvinceThe old walled town of Galle and a string of beaches along the southwest coast.4 places
  • GampahaWestern ProvinceThe district around the international airport, with the beach town of Negombo.2 places
  • HambantotaSouthern ProvinceThe dry southeast, with leopards in Yala and long empty beaches around Tangalle.2 places
  • JaffnaNorthern ProvinceThe Tamil heartland of the far north, with a large Dutch fort, grand temples and its own food.2 places
  • KalutaraWestern ProvinceA stretch of the west coast south of Colombo, marked by a great white stupa beside the river.1 place
  • KandyCentral ProvinceThe last royal capital, built around a lake in the hills and home to the Temple of the Tooth.3 places
  • KegalleSabaragamuwa ProvinceGreen foothills between Colombo and the highlands, known for rafting at Kitulgala.1 place
  • KilinochchiNorthern ProvinceA quiet district on the road to Jaffna, with lagoons and open farmland.Places coming soon
  • KurunegalaNorth Western ProvinceA busy crossroads district of coconut land and rock fortresses, with few tourists.1 place
  • MannarNorthern ProvinceA dry, flat corner of the northwest, with a Portuguese and Dutch fort and old baobab trees.1 place
  • MataleCentral ProvinceA district of two climates, from the hot rock of Sigiriya to the cool, misty ridges of the Knuckles.3 places
  • MataraSouthern ProvinceThe southern tip of the island, with Mirissa's whale season and the surf of Weligama.3 places
  • MonaragalaUva ProvinceA large, quiet district in the southeast, and home to the holy town of Kataragama.3 places
  • MullaitivuNorthern ProvinceA quiet stretch of the northeast coast, with lagoons and long beaches.Places coming soon
  • Nuwara EliyaCentral ProvinceThe high tea country, with cool air, old bungalows and the plateau of Horton Plains.3 places
  • PolonnaruwaNorth Central ProvinceA medieval royal capital and the elephant country around Minneriya.2 places
  • PuttalamNorth Western ProvinceThe northwest coast, from the kitesurfing beaches of Kalpitiya to the forests of Wilpattu.2 places
  • RatnapuraSabaragamuwa ProvinceThe gem city district, reaching from the rainforest of Sinharaja to the elephants of Udawalawe.3 places
  • TrincomaleeEastern ProvinceA deep natural harbour and pale beaches, at their best from about April to September.2 places
  • VavuniyaNorthern ProvinceThe gateway to the north, on the road and railway toward Jaffna.Places coming soon
