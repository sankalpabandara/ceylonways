Sri Lanka Highlights

The classic first trip: Sigiriya, Kandy, the hill country and Galle.

Airport → Sigiriya → Kandy → Ella → Galle → Airport

From US$779 per person, two adults sharing. Sample estimate in USD, before seasonal changes. Flights not included.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Sigiriya and Dambulla

    Drive from the airport, about 4 h

    • Dambulla cave temple

  2. Day 2

    Sigiriya and Dambulla

    • Minneriya or Kaudulla jeep safari
    • Climb Sigiriya Rock

  3. Day 3

    Kandy

    Drive from Sigiriya, about 2 h 30 min

    • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

  4. Day 4

    Ella

    Scenic train from Kandy, about 7 h

    Free time.

  5. Day 5

    Ella

    • Little Adam's Peak walk
    • Nine Arch Bridge walk

  6. Day 6

    Galle

    Drive from Ella, about 5 h 24 min

    • Galle Fort walking tour

    Departure

    Drive from Galle, about 2 h 18 min
