Where you stay

Rail update. This sample route uses a train, but parts of the hill country line were still closed after Cyclone Ditwah when we last checked in November 2026. A car may replace some or all of it. See getting around.

Sample data. These are sample stay types used to price the trip, not named hotels. Ratings shown are sample values.

  • Negombo, 1 nightBeachfront boutique hotel. Boutique hotel. Breakfast. On the beach, 30 minutes from the airport.
  • Sigiriya and Dambulla, 3 nightsRock view resort. Resort. Half board. View of Sigiriya Rock.
  • Kandy, 2 nightsRiver valley resort. Resort. Breakfast. 30 minutes outside the city.
  • Ella, 2 nightsTea hill boutique hotel. Boutique hotel. Breakfast. 5 minutes by tuk tuk from town.
  • Tissamaharama for Yala, 1 nightWild coast safari lodge. Eco lodge. Half board. Next to the park boundary.
  • Mirissa and Weligama, 2 nightsPalm garden resort. Resort. Breakfast. On the beach.
