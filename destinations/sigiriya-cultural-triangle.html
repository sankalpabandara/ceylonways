Sigiriya and the Cultural Triangle

A rock fortress, painted caves and ruined royal cities in the dry heart of the island.

Add to my tripSee packages
Photo: Sander Don, Unsplash

Sigiriya Rock rises about 200 metres above the forest. King Kashyapa built a palace on top in the fifth century. Today you climb past the mirror wall and the lion paws to the summit. Go at opening time, when it is cooler and quieter.

Close by are the Dambulla cave temples, filled with Buddha statues and painted ceilings, and the ancient city of Polonnaruwa, which is easy to see by bicycle. Anuradhapura, the oldest capital, is a longer day trip.

From about July to October, hundreds of elephants gather around the reservoirs at Minneriya and Kaudulla. A late afternoon jeep safari is the usual way to see them.

Weather

Dry and hot for much of the year. Heavier rain usually comes from October to December.
