Ella and the Hill Country

Tea estates, cool mornings, waterfalls and the train ride people talk about for years.

Add to my tripSee packages
Photo: V. Epiney, CC BY-SA 2.0, resized

The hill country is a world of tea. Nuwara Eliya is high and cool, with old bungalows and tea factories you can visit. Horton Plains starts before dawn and ends at World's End, a cliff with a long view south if the clouds stay away.

Ella is smaller and greener. Walk up Little Adam's Peak, see a train cross the Nine Arch Bridge, or hike Ella Rock with a guide. Waterfalls such as Ravana and Diyaluma are close by.

The train from Kandy to Ella usually takes about seven hours and passes tea estates the whole way. Cyclone Ditwah closed the line in late 2025, and only the stretch from Nanu Oya through Ella to Badulla had reopened when we last checked in November 2026, so check before you book.

Things you can add in the planner

  • Horton Plains and World's End walk6 h, Very early start, about US$45 per adult (sample)Leave at 5 am. 9 km loop walk. Clouds often roll in by 10 am.
  • Little Adam's Peak walk2 h, Sunrise or late afternoon, free
  • Nine Arch Bridge walk1.5 h, Morning, freeCheck train times to see one cross.
