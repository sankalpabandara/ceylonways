Wildlife and Rainforests

Leopards in Yala, elephants in Udawalawe, and the old rainforest of Sinharaja.

Add to my tripSee packages
How long
One or two nights per park
Safari length
Half day or full day
Best months
February to July for Yala
Good for
Wildlife, birding, photographers

Yala is the best known park, with a good chance of leopards along with sloth bears, crocodiles and many birds. Part of the park usually closes for a few weeks around September. Go at dawn and expect busy tracks in high season.

Udawalawe is the easiest place to see elephants, often on every drive. Wilpattu in the northwest is quieter, with leopards in thick forest. Minneriya is known for its elephant gathering from July to October.

Sinharaja is a protected rainforest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Most of its birds and many of its frogs and lizards live nowhere else. Walk with a local guide in the morning and bring leech socks in wet months.
