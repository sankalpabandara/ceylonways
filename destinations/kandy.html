Kandy

The last royal capital, built around a lake and home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

Add to my tripSee packages
Photo: McKay Savage from Chennai, India, CC BY 2.0, resized

Kandy sits in the hills between the cultural north and the tea country, so most journeys pass through. The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic holds a relic of the Buddha. Evening ceremonies with drums are the most atmospheric time to visit.

Walk around Kandy Lake, spend a morning at the Royal Botanic Gardens in Peradeniya, and catch a Kandyan dance show. In July or August the Esala Perahera brings nights of processions with dancers and elephants. Rooms fill early and cost more during the festival.

Kandy is also where many people start the hill country train ride toward Nuwara Eliya and Ella, when the line is open. Parts of it were still closed after Cyclone Ditwah when we last checked in November 2026.
