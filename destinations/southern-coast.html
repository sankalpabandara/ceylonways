The Southern Coast

Galle Fort, surf bays and quiet beaches, with whales offshore in the dry season.

Add to my tripSee packages
Photo: Abishek Palraj, CC BY-SA 4.0, resized

Galle Fort is a walled old town by the sea, first built by the Portuguese and expanded by the Dutch. Walk the ramparts at sunset and stay inside the walls to enjoy it after the day visitors leave.

Further east, Unawatuna, Weligama, Mirissa and Tangalle each have their own feel. Weligama has gentle waves for surf lessons. Mirissa is the base for whale watching, mostly from November to April.

The south coast is at its best from December to April. From May to September the sea is rough and many beaches are not safe for swimming.
