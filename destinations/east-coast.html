The East Coast

Wide beaches, coral reefs and calm seas from April to September, when the south coast is rough.

Add to my tripSee packages
How long
Two to four nights
From Sigiriya
About 2 hours by car
Best months
April to September
Good for
Snorkelling, calm swimming, surf

The east coast has its good weather in the middle of the year. Trincomalee has a deep natural harbour, the Koneswaram temple on Swami Rock, and the beaches of Nilaveli and Uppuveli.

Pigeon Island National Park, a short boat ride from Nilaveli, is one of the easiest places in Sri Lanka to snorkel over coral and see reef sharks. Further south, Arugam Bay is a surf town with its best waves from about May to September.

From November to January the northeast monsoon brings heavy rain. Some hotels close and boat trips stop.

What to see

  • Nilaveli beach

Weather
