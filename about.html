About Ceylonways

Ceylonways helps visitors plan a trip to Sri Lanka that fits their budget, their interests and their dates.

Most tour sites sell fixed packages. We start with a good package and let you change it, with the cost and the driving time in view the whole way.

What works today

  • Destination and travel guides
  • Six starting packages
  • A planner where you can change every part of a trip and see the cost
  • Saving, sharing and downloading your plan

What is not ready yet

  • Live hotel rates and availability
  • Google ratings and reviews
  • Sending a quote request to our team
  • Payments and bookings

We will add these as agreements with hotels and drivers are in place. We will not show reviews, awards or partners we do not have.

Your Sri Lanka. Your way. Within your budget.
